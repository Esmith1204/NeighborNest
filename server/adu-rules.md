# ADU rules summary

Simplified placeholder for demo. Not legal advice. Verify with your city planning department and HCD.

- State law requires cities to allow ADUs on most residential lots.
- ADUs generally can be assigned their own address.
- Fees and setbacks vary by city.
- Permits are required.
- Financing options vary by lender.

Source for the state rules: HCD ADU Handbook (March 2026),
https://www.hcd.ca.gov/sites/default/files/docs/policy-and-research/adu-handbook-update.pdf
(listed in the event's docs/background.md; not read in full by this team). ADU law is now
Gov Code 66310 to 66342, renumbered from 65852.2 by SB 477; older city documents may still use
the old numbers.
